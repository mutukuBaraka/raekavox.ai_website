Built in Kenya, for Kenyan businesses

RaekaVox AI started from a simple frustration: too many important calls go unanswered. We’re building the digital receptionist that local SMEs actually need.

The founding team

Two co-founders combining local market insight with strong software execution.

Baraka Mutuku Mutinda

Founder & CEO

Baraka leads product direction, customer discovery and early sales. He holds a Diploma in Automotive Engineering from The Nairobi National Polytechnic. His experience on the frontline of customer service taught him how Kenyan clients actually communicate when they need help.

He experienced the problem personally — difficulty reaching hospitals and doctors by phone — and is determined to fix it for small businesses across the country.

Based in Nakuru / Naivasha · LinkedIn: baraka-mutinda-702b3139a

Kennedy Wafukho Wekesa

Co-Founder & Technical Lead

Kennedy is a Full Stack Web Developer with a BSc in Computer Science (Software Development) from Kenyatta University (2016–2020).

He currently works as Frontend Web Developer at CEMES Ltd and previously spent over two years as Software Developer at BalancedKPI. He brings production experience with React.js, JavaScript, PHP/Laravel, MySQL, Docker and Jenkins — and leads the architecture and delivery of the RaekaVox voice platform.

Based in Nairobi · GitHub: github.com/Wekesir
