How RaekaVox AI works

A simple flow designed for real Kenyan businesses — from the first ring to a confirmed booking or payment.

Call comes in

Your existing business number rings. RaekaVox answers within two rings — day or night.

Understands the caller

Handles Kenyan English, Swahili and Sheng. Asks the right questions to understand the request.

Takes action

Books an appointment, answers FAQs, takes a message, or starts an M-Pesa STK Push.

You stay in control

Get a clear summary of every call. Escalate to a human when needed.
